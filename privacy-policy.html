Privacy Policy – ProstoMaslo Pin Publisher

Last updated: November 12, 2026

ProstoMaslo Pin Publisher ("the App") is a personal tool operated by ProstoMaslo (http://minestar.com.ua). It publishes and schedules Pins to the operator's own Pinterest account through the official Pinterest API.

1. Data we access

With the account owner's permission (OAuth), the App accesses the following Pinterest data:

  • Basic account info (username) to confirm the connection.
  • The account's boards and board sections, used to pick where Pins are published.
  • Pins created by the App (title, description, link, alt text, image).

The App does not access other users' data, messages, followers, passwords or payment information.

2. How data is used

The data is used only to create, publish and schedule Pins on the owner's own account. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, profiling or for training models.

3. Storage

OAuth tokens, the queue of scheduled Pins and the images waiting to be published are stored only on the owner's local computer. Scheduled images are deleted after they are published. Finished queue records are deleted after 30 days. The App has no server and no database, and it does not send data to third parties. The only exception is Pinterest itself, through its API.

4. Third parties

Data is sent only to Pinterest (api.pinterest.com) to perform the actions described above. Pinterest's own privacy policy applies: https://policy.pinterest.com/privacy-policy

5. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest → Settings → Security and logins → Connected apps. Deleting the App's local folder removes all stored tokens and data.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
